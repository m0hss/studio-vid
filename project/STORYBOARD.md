# Fixbyte Studio — "Your Name Here" (30.00 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps)

## Concept
Every template ships with a placeholder. One of Fixbyte's own designs (Common Grounds)
literally has *"Your Name Here"* handwritten on its coffee cup. The film takes that literally:
a text cursor travels through Fixbyte's real, live designs and types a local owner's name into
each one, in that design's own typography. Inside the designs the cursor and the text selection take
the colour of the design's own text; on the dark brand scenes the cursor is Fixbyte teal. In the drop,
the cursor becomes the "i" of **Fixbyte Studio** (set in Aller Bold, as the Studio logo), and both i's
take the brand diamond as their dot.

Designs were captured from the live demo pages linked on studio.fixbyte.be; the names were
typed into the real DOM keystroke by keystroke (assets/designs/*_tNN.webp), so every frame
of the "typing" is the actual site rendering the new name (tools/capture/). The businesses are fictional.

## Music
`gs4u_syntheticwarpath.mp3`, 120 BPM (bar = 2.000 s).
- Film 0.00–28.00 = track 135.955–163.955 (breakdown → layer at 8 → hit 12 → riser 16 → snare 23.5, gap, DROP 24.00)
- Film 28.00–30.00 = track 191.955–193.955 (the track's own drop-to-outro release), faded out 28.6–30.0
- Spliced on a bar line; mastered to −14 LUFS integrated, ≤ −1.5 dBTP.

## Sound design
Typing ticks and select blips on each keystroke; whooshes on the three pushes; a soft
message chime; a reverse swell into the drop and a sub hit under it. All synthesized
(tools/build_audio.py), mixed under the music.
